Smiles is a newly established dental office in Boston, Massachusetts, with a dedicated and professional team 
of dentists and specialists committed to delivering high-quality care at an affordable price.

Goals and Objectives Despite having a small client base, Smiles is focused on achieving the following goals: 
 -Implementing a secure and organized database system to efficiently manage and store client information.
  -Analyzing trends and insights to understand what drives people to choose Smiles, with the aim of expanding its client base.


On this project, I created the database and generated insights to better understand trends and identify opportunities to increase the client base and uncover what drives patients to choose Smiles. To support this analysis, the following areas were explored through SQL queries:
Dentist specializations: To understand the range of services offered and the demand for different specialties.
Confirmed appointments: To identify trends and patterns in scheduled and completed visits.
Canceled appointments: To analyze cancellation patterns and uncover potential factors contributing to missed visits, informing strategies to reduce them.
Appointments and doctors: To assess workload distribution and performance across providers.
Appointments, patients, and patient city location: To identify geographic trends and potential areas for growth.
